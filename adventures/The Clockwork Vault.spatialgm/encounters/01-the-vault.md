A dwarven vault cut into a mountain. Its gears still turn; its guardians still listen.

## Antechamber
A round brass door fills the north wall, its lock a nest of tumblers the size of your thumb.

## Gear Hall
Gears taller than a house turn slowly overhead. Brass figures stand in alcoves along the walls — statues, surely.

## Steam Gallery
A narrow walkway over a pit of hissing vents. The air is wet and scalding.

## Vault
Gold, stacked in neat dwarven bricks — and a brass giant, kneeling, with its eyes beginning to glow.
