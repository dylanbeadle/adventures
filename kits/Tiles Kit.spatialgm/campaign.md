# Tiles Kit

Floors, walls, stairs and arches for building boards, and the original roof and door pieces. A kit (D139): take its pieces from the Bookshelf into your campaign, or paste a TaleSpire slab that uses them (each piece's `same-as` is its TaleSpire catalogue id). Its models are the library's own USD files in `media/`, sharing one material library; Spatial GM makes a piece's self-contained USDZ, with only the textures it uses, when you take it (D146).
