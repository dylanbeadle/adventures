A letter reaches you at the inn, sealed with a merchant's wax. Goblins from the hill took a
strongbox on the Low Road last night. The merchant, Vell, wants it back unopened.

## GM
Vell is honest and frightened. If the heroes ask the oracle about him, he really is who he says.
Add "Bring back Vell's strongbox" to Threads when the heroes accept.
